## If something goes wrong

- **A refusal or a crash before any 2024 number is printed:** nothing has been seen. Fix it through a commit (reviewed, on the branch), and start again at step 2 with the new SHA.
- **Once a 2024 number is printed, it's final.** No rerun with a change, no other model, no other threshold. A crash after that point: write down what happened, and the report says so.
- **Never:** another `--split test` run after step 4, a change judged on 2024, or a pick made after seeing it.
